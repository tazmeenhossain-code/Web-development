The Bottlenose Dolphin

Dolphins are highly intelligent marine mammals known for their playful behavior and friendly nature.
They live in social groups called pods and communicate using clicks and whistles.

Fun Facts About Dolphins:

  • Dolphins sleep with only half of their brain at a time so they can keep breathing.
  • They use echolocation to find food and navigate underwater.
  • Dolphins have two stomachs: one for storing food and one for digesting it.

How We Can Protect Dolphins:

  1. Reduce single-use plastics to keep the oceans clean and safe.
  2. Support sustainable fishing practices that prevent dolphins from getting caught in nets.
  3. Keep a safe distance when boating in areas where dolphins live.
