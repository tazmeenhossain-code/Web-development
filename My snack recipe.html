Berry Banana Smoothie

Ingredients

  • 1 ripe banana
  • 1 cup of mixed berries
  • 1 cup of milk or yogurt
  • 1 tablespoon of honey

Steps

  1. Peel the banana and slice it into pieces.
  2. Put the banana, berries, milk, and honey into a blender.
  3. Ask a grown-up to help you turn on the blender until smooth.
  4. Pour the smoothie into a glass and enjoy!
